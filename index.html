Rules

  1. Fill every cell with a sun or a moon.
  2. Each row and each column holds the same number of suns and moons (3 of each on the 6×6 grid).
  3. No more than two equal symbols may sit next to each other in a row or column.
  4. Cells joined by = hold the same symbol. Cells joined by × hold different symbols.
  5. Shaded cells are given at the start and cannot be changed.

Controls

  1. Tap a cell to cycle blank → sun → moon. The Sun and Moon modes make a tap toggle only that symbol.
  2. Keyboard: arrow keys move, S sun, M moon, Space cycles, Backspace clears, Z/Y undo/redo.
  3. Cells are named by row and column, for example R3C5 is row 3, column 5.

How difficulty is rated

Each puzzle is solved by a reasoning engine that always uses the simplest technique available. The rating depends on the hardest techniques it needed and how often it needed them.

Trivial
A sign next to a filled cell, or a row or column that already has all of one symbol.
Basic
No three in a row: two equal symbols side by side, or with a gap between them, force the neighbouring cell.
Simple
Line analysis with up to 4 open cells: only a few ways to finish the row or column, and they all agree on a cell.
Intermediate
Line analysis with 5 to 7 open cells.
Advanced
Line analysis with 8 or more open cells.
Contradiction
Assume a cell and follow forced steps (up to Simple) until a rule breaks.
Deep contradiction
A contradiction chain that needs Intermediate or Advanced steps along the way.

All levels use a 6×6 grid; difficulty comes from the reasoning required. Easy puzzles 1–20 are warm-ups that need only signs, counts and no three in a row; puzzles 21–50 add a few short line checks. Medium needs longer line checks, on rows or columns with 5 or more open cells. Hard needs one to three short contradiction chains (up to 4 steps). Master needs several contradiction chains and starts with very few filled cells.

Master · Dare to try

These puzzles cannot be finished with pattern-spotting alone. Several times per puzzle you will need to assume a cell, follow what it forces, and find where it breaks a rule.

They start with very few filled cells. Most players will want Hard first.
